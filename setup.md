1. Policy Management Module
Core Features
•	Policy Violation Reports 
o	Log and track all violations 
o	Filter by branch, user, policy type, date 
o	Status tracking (Pending → Investigating → Resolved) 
o	Attach evidence (documents, screenshots) 
o	Auto-escalation for repeated offenders 
•	Policy Clarification (Ticketing System) 
o	Staff can submit questions about policies 
o	Categorize by policy type (HR, Finance, Operations) 
o	Assign to compliance/admin team 
o	SLA tracking (response time) 
o	Public knowledge base from answered questions

Add More Features (This is where it becomes powerful)
•	Policy of the Day 
o	Random or scheduled policy highlight on dashboard 
o	Short, digestible format 
o	Track who viewed/read it 
•	Policy Acknowledgement Tracking 
o	Require staff to confirm they’ve read policies 
o	Digital sign-off with timestamps 
o	Useful for audits 
•	Version Control 
o	Track policy updates and history 
o	Notify staff when a policy changes 
•	Policy Risk Scoring 
o	Identify high-risk branches/users based on violations 
o	Helps management focus attention 
•	Automated Alerts 
o	Notify managers when violations occur 
o	Weekly compliance summary reports
