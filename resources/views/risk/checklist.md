PURPOSE
This checklist enables any supervising officer, manager, auditor, or any authorized person, even without a finance background, to identify early warning signs of theft or fraud before losses grow large. This edition reflects the institution's cashless operations policy: all transactions are conducted exclusively through the Withinhere branch wallet or the Whence Financial Services app. No branch may hold or handle physical cash at any time.

HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT
•	Work through each section in order. Do not skip items even if the branch looks fine.
•	Tick ✓ (pass) or ✗ (fail) in the last column of each row.
•	For every ✗, write what you observed in the Finding / Notes column.
•	After completing all sections, calculate your Risk Score using the guide on the next page.
•	If you encounter anything you do not understand, stop and call any of the following before continuing: Provincial Manager, Accountant, Risk Manager, Manager Administration, or Technical Director.

RISK SCORING GUIDE

After completing all checklist sections, count the total number of ✗ (fail) items and use the table below to determine the branch's overall risk rating.

Risk Level	Score	What It Means	Required Action
🟢 LOW	0–3 ✗ items	Branch is compliant	Standard monitoring; next audit in 3 months
🟡 MEDIUM	4–7 ✗ items	Weaknesses present	Action plan within 7 days; follow-up in 30 days
🔴 HIGH	8–12 ✗ items	Significant control failures	Escalate to Regional Manager; freeze lending if needed
🚨 CRITICAL	13+ ✗ OR any confirmed fraud	Immediate risk to institution	Suspend operations, summon Risk & Finance teams within 24 hrs.

IMPORTANT: Even a single confirmed fraud finding immediately elevates the rating to CRITICAL, regardless of total score. Always check the Fraud Risk Indicators section (Section 5) before finalizing your rating.

SECTION 2 — WITHINHERE WALLET & DIGITAL PAYMENT CONTROLS

ZERO-CASH POLICY: This institution does not permit any physical cash to be held or handled at branch level at any time. All client payments must be received through the Withinhere app, the Whence Financial Services app, or the company mobile money lines. All loan disbursements must be made exclusively through the Withinhere branch wallet. A payment received via mobile money is NOT confirmed until it has been transferred into the Withinhere branch wallet. Only the Branch Manager is authorized to initiate this transfer, and it must occur in the same transaction (immediately the money is received), no delay is permitted.

This section verifies that the branch is operating in full compliance with the cashless policy and that the Withinhere wallet is being used correctly as the single authorized channel for all money movement.

Zero physical cash confirmed at branch	No cash of any kind, coins, notes, petty cash, or any float, should be present anywhere at the branch at any time. The institution's policy prohibits all physical cash handling by branch staff.	Physically inspect the branch: till drawers, desks, safe (if any), reception area, and personal bags/wallets of staff on duty. If any cash is found, record the amount, location, and the name of the person holding it.	Any physical cash found at the branch , regardless of the amount or the explanation given,  is an immediate red flag and must be escalated to the Provincial Manager and Risk Manager the same day.		
All client payments received via authorized channels only	Clients must pay through one of three authorized channels only: (1) the Withinhere app, (2) the Whence Financial Services app, or (3) the company mobile money lines.
