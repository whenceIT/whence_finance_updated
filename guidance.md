Following our review of the Motor Vehicle Loans system and the subsequent consultations around how we intend to manage the product going forward, I have consolidated the various recommendations and operational requirements into one document for your attention.

The intention is to have the system manage the entire lifecycle of a Motor Vehicle Loan, rather than only capturing the loan and repayment side of the transaction.

Among the key areas that need to be accommodated are:

Detailed borrower/KYC information, including PEP and sanctions screening;
Clear identification of the staff member responsible for each loan, the originating branch, branch-level assessor, District and Province;
Configurable loan bands, interest rates, tenure, service fees and approval levels;
Branch referral and Loan Consultant incentive tracking, with incentives only becoming payable upon full settlement;
Different requirements for individually owned vehicles, vehicles supported by Letters of Sale and corporate-owned vehicles;
Vehicle valuation, insurance, inspection and photographic records;
Proper vehicle custody and storage management, including the exact location where each vehicle is parked, details of the owner of the house/garage, contact details and alternative contacts;
A complete movement history where a vehicle is transferred from one storage location to another;
Weekly physical roll-calls of all vehicles in custody, supported by current photographs;
Automated client SMS notifications and Management alerts;
Re-loan and Add-On/Top-Up management;
Traffic offence settlement facilities where applicable;
Structured default and recovery management, including applicable timelines, penalties and Notices of Intention to Sell;
Complete disposal management where a vehicle has to be sold;
Capture of the eventual buyer’s full identification and contact details;
Automatic generation of a Vehicle Purchase Report confirming, among other things, that the vehicle has been purchased in its existing condition on an “as is” basis;
Proper allocation and reporting of proceeds received from the sale of a vehicle;
A complete audit trail showing every officer who handled, assessed, approved, moved, released or disposed of a vehicle/loan; and
A Management dashboard giving us visibility over the portfolio by staff member, branch, district, province, loan performance, vehicles in custody, defaults, recoveries, disposals and overall profitability.
Most importantly, I would like us to get to a point where Management can open any Motor Vehicle Loan and immediately establish the entire history:

Client → Responsible Staff Member → Branch → Branch-Level Assessor → District → Province → KYC/Ownership Verification → Valuation → Approval → Vehicle Intake → Storage Location → Custodian → Weekly Verification → Disbursement → Repayment → Default/Recovery where applicable → Disposal → Buyer → Final Closure.

We should not have a situation where a loan or vehicle is on the system but we still have to call different people to establish who originated it, who assessed it, where the vehicle is parked, who is keeping it, how the loan is performing or what eventually happened to the vehicle.

The commercial parameters such as interest rates, loan bands, fees, penalties and timelines should, as much as possible, be configurable rather than permanently hard-coded, so that we are able to make future Management-approved changes without having to redevelop the system each time.
